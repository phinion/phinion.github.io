## Overview

Ashborn was an ambitious 3D action RPG developed by Fireslug Studios, inspired by games such as *Final Fantasy Crystal Chronicles*.

The game combined hack-and-slash combat, dungeon exploration and village interactions, with players venturing into nearby dungeons to complete quests, defeat enemies and protect their village.

This was my first professional role after university, working remotely with a multidisciplinary team of over 15 developers and creatives.

I initially joined as a Unity Developer before taking on additional responsibilities and eventually being promoted to **Lead Programmer**.

## My Contributions

As Lead Programmer, I helped coordinate the programming team while continuing to design and implement core gameplay systems using Unity and C#.

Some of my main contributions included:

- **Combat Architecture:** Refined and expanded a timing-based combat system featuring attack phases, weapon combinations and modular abilities.
- **Spell & VFX Integration:** Developed a ScriptableObject-based system for configuring and adding spells to character movesets.
- **Gameplay Systems:** Implemented inventory, dialogue and character interaction systems.
- **World Navigation:** Improved the hex-based world map and implemented level transitions.
- **NPC Generation:** Created a villager randomisation system using modular character assets.
- **Interactive Audio:** Worked with Wwise to integrate dynamic music that responded to combat intensity and player health.
- **Technical Leadership:** Coordinated development tasks, mentored programmers, supported recruitment and collaborated with other disciplines.

## Outcome

Although Ashborn never reached a commercial release, it was an incredibly valuable experience that shaped my career as a game developer.

It gave me the opportunity to develop complex gameplay systems, work closely with talented artists and musicians, and grow into a technical leadership role.

I'm particularly proud of the work our team accomplished and the creative ambition behind the project.
